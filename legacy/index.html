FAQ

Simply put.

The questions we hear most. Something else on your mind? Write to us on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need a subscription? +

Each device includes a SIM card and the first month free. After that, a small monthly plan keeps the tracker connected. You can cancel anytime.

Is installation difficult? +

No. The Plug model goes into the diagnostic port under your dashboard. The Pro model connects to 12V/24V power with 3 wires — a step-by-step guide is included, or any auto electrician can do it in 15 minutes.

Will it drain my car battery? +

No. Trackers use less than 5 mA in sleep mode and switch to low-power automatically when the car is parked.

Can I track more than one car? +

Yes. Add as many devices as you like to one account and see them all on the same map — ideal for families and fleets.

What if a thief finds the tracker? +

Our devices are small enough to hide almost anywhere. If a wired unit is disconnected, you get an instant alert with its last known location.
